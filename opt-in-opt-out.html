Opt-In & Opt-Out

Last updated: February 2025. This page describes how you consent to and can stop receiving ZarchTalk reminders, in line with toll-free and application-to-person (A2P) messaging compliance.

About the program

ZarchTalk (sender: Zarchbot) is an automated reminder service that sends SMS text messages and/or voice calls about upcoming events (e.g., youth group meetings and activities). Messages and calls are sent only to people who have opted in; the service is managed by the organization that runs it.

You must opt in before we send you anything. We do not send reminders or place reminder calls until you have given clear consent. If you have not opted in, you will not receive messages or calls from this service.

Opt-in (how to sign up)

You opt in by:

  • Providing your phone number to the organization that operates ZarchTalk (e.g., your ward or branch), and
  • Giving clear consent to receive SMS and/or voice reminders.

Consent is collected directly by the organization. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier. Reply HELP for help.

Opt-out (how to stop)

Any reasonable method of asking to stop will opt you out. We will honor requests to unsubscribe whether you contact the organization, reply to a message, or use the methods below.

  • SMS: Reply STOP to any reminder text to unsubscribe. We also automatically opt you out if you text UNSUBSCRIBE (or we detect that word in your reply). You may also contact the organization that runs the service and ask to be removed.
  • Voice: Contact the organization or administrator that operates ZarchTalk to stop voice reminders. We will also automatically opt you out from voice reminders if we detect you saying unsubscribe during a voice call. There is no in-call STOP keyword required; any reasonable request to stop will be honored.

Once you opt out, you will no longer receive reminders unless you opt in again.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Reminders are sent based on the event schedule (e.g., in the 24 hours before an event). You may receive one or a few messages or calls per week depending on how many events are scheduled. The service does not send marketing or promotional content.

Questions

For opt-in, opt-out, or consent questions, contact the organization or administrator that operates the ZarchTalk Service.
