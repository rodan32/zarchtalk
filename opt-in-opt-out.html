Opt-In & Opt-Out

Last updated: February 2025. This page describes how you consent to and can stop receiving ZarchTalk reminders, in line with toll-free and application-to-person (A2P) messaging compliance.

About the program

ZarchTalk is an automated reminder service that sends SMS text messages and/or voice calls about upcoming events (e.g., youth group meetings and activities). Messages are sent only to people who have opted in and are managed by the organization that runs the service.

Opt-in (how to sign up)

You opt in by:

  • Providing your phone number to the organization that operates ZarchTalk (e.g., your ward or branch), and
  • Giving clear consent to receive SMS and/or voice reminders.

Consent is collected directly by the organization. You will not receive reminders until you have opted in. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

Opt-out (how to stop)

You can opt out at any time:

  • SMS: Reply STOP to any reminder text to unsubscribe from SMS. You may also contact the organization that runs the service and ask to be removed.
  • Voice: Contact the organization or administrator that operates ZarchTalk and ask to stop voice reminders. There is no in-call STOP; opt-out is handled by the organization.

Once you opt out, you will no longer receive reminders unless you opt in again.

Message frequency

Reminders are sent based on the event schedule (e.g., in the 24 hours before an event). You may receive one or a few messages or calls per week depending on how many events are scheduled. The service does not send marketing or promotional content.

Questions

For opt-in, opt-out, or consent questions, contact the organization or administrator that operates the ZarchTalk Service.
