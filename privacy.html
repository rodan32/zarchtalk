Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025. This is a draft for review.

This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with the ZarchTalk text and phone reminder service (“Service”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: Name, phone number, and contact type (e.g., youth or parent) that you or your organization provide for sending reminders.
  • Consent preferences: Whether you have agreed to receive SMS and/or voice reminders.
  • Reminder logs: Records of when reminders were sent (e.g., event, date, delivery method) for operation and support.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Send scheduled SMS and voice reminders for events (e.g., youth group meetings and activities).
  • Honor your consent choices (SMS only, voice only, or both).
  • Operate, debug, and improve the Service.

3. Data Storage and Third Parties

Contact and reminder data may be stored in a local database. Reminders are delivered via third-party services (e.g., Twilio for SMS and voice). Calendar/event data may be read from tools such as Google Sheets if configured. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You may request to stop receiving reminders, update your contact details, or request deletion of your data by contacting the organization that manages the Service. We will process such requests in line with applicable law.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information (e.g., secure storage and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the organization or administrator that operates the ZarchTalk Service.
